The garden needs you

A storm has blown litter all over the garden, and your marble is the clean-up helper. Bounce into the rubbish to pick it up, and collect it in order from 10 to 50 for a 500-point clean sweep.

Be gentle with the mole. This is his home, and if you bump him, all your litter spills and your score drops to zero. Steer clear of the red mushroom too. It's toxic, and every touch costs 50 points.

Tap the left or right side of the board to flip. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys.
